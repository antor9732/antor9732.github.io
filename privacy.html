HomeBudget privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

HomeBudget ("the app") is a household budget app for Android. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

Data stored on your phone

Your household's transactions, categories, budgets and member list are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Android cloud backup of app data is turned off.

Google account and Google Drive

If you choose "Continue with Google", the app uses Google Sign-In to learn your Google account's email address, name and account ID, so it can identify you as a household member. The app requests the Google Drive permission drive.file, which only allows it to access files that the app itself creates, or that you choose in Google's file picker. The app cannot see any other files in your Google Drive.

The app creates one file named "HomeBudget - <household name>.homebudget.json" in the household owner's Google Drive. It contains the household name, the owner's email, the currency, the household encryption key and the household's changes. Amounts, titles, notes and member details in the changes are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. When the owner invites a family member by email, the app shares this file with that Google account using Google Drive sharing. Only the owner and the invited accounts can open it. If the owner sets an optional recovery passphrase, the household key is additionally protected by that passphrase.

Use of Google user data

Google user data is used only to provide the app's features: identifying household members and saving and synchronizing the household file in Google Drive. HomeBudget's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to anyone except as needed to provide these features through Google's own services.

No HomeBudget server, no tracking

The app communicates only with Google's sign-in and Google Drive services. It has no ads, analytics or third-party trackers. The developer does not receive or store your data.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Remove household from this phone to delete the data on a phone. Delete the household file in your Google Drive to delete the cloud copy. You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is intended for adults managing household finances.
