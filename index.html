HomeBudget

HomeBudget is an Android app for a family's income, expenses and monthly budget in Bangladeshi Taka. It works offline, and family members can share one household through the owner's own Google Drive. There is no HomeBudget server, no ads and no tracking.

  • Sign in with Google; your household is created and saved to your Google Drive automatically.
  • Invite family members by Gmail; they join by opening the shared household file in the app.
  • Itemized entries (e.g. a monthly bazar list), budget alerts, monthly and yearly reports.
